Privacy Policy — BLINK

Effective date: November 3, 2026

BLINK is a mobile game developed and published by BIG NOSE STUDIOS.

This Privacy Policy explains how BLINK handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

BLINK does not require users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone access, or camera access.

However, BLINK uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical and device-related information necessary to provide advertising, measure performance, detect errors, and operate the application.

This information may include:

  • Device and operating system information
  • App version
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from technical information
  • App interactions and usage information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and crash information

2. Advertising

BLINK displays advertisements, including rewarded ads and interstitial ads.

BLINK uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, to deliver advertising.

Depending on the user's location, consent choices, device settings, and applicable laws, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising identifiers, and interactions with ads in order to:

  • Deliver advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Limit the frequency of advertisements
  • Provide personalized or non-personalized advertising where permitted

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, BLINK uses consent mechanisms provided through Google advertising services before requesting or using data for personalized advertising.

Users may be able to modify or withdraw certain advertising privacy choices through the privacy options available within the application or through their device settings.

4. App Usage and Diagnostics

BLINK may process limited technical information related to application performance and stability.

This information may be used to:

  • Identify technical errors
  • Diagnose crashes
  • Improve app stability
  • Improve gameplay performance
  • Protect the application against abuse or malfunction

5. Information We Do Not Request

BLINK does not require access to:

  • Contacts
  • Precise GPS location
  • Photos or photo library
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • SMS messages
  • Call logs
  • Health information
  • Financial information
  • Biometric information

BLINK also does not require users to create an account or provide login credentials.

6. Third-Party Services

BLINK may use third-party services necessary for advertising, analytics, diagnostics, hosting, or technical operation.

The primary advertising provider currently used by BLINK is:

Google AdMob / Google LLC

BIG NOSE STUDIOS does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers only where necessary to provide advertising, application functionality, diagnostics, security, or other technical services.

8. Data Retention

BIG NOSE STUDIOS does not maintain user accounts or a database of directly identifying personal information for BLINK.

9. Data Security

10. Children's Privacy

BLINK is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.
